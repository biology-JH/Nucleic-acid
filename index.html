04HISTORY IN TIME · LESSONS IN SYLLABUS ORDER

1909

Levene proposes a repeating four-base DNA structure: the tetranucleotide hypothesis.

1948–1950

Chargaff’s measurements show that DNA base composition varies among species.

1952

Hershey and Chase use radioisotopes to test whether DNA or protein enters bacterial cells.
